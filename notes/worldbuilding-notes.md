# Project Notes

## Worldbuilding notes

- The Hunt is not merely a monster-force. It is a memory and an absence.
- The Promise is the heart of the world, but it cannot be reduced to a single simple answer.
- The Isle was made from survival and commitment. It remembers the people who arrived and the promises they kept.
- The central tree is both a landmark and a living myth.
- The roots under the world are not a dungeon. They are the buried structure of the world’s meaning.

## Design notes

- The Discord should lead people into the world, not merely explain it.
- Player experience should feel like discovery rather than instruction.
- The world should reward questions, not just progression.
- The project should live in tension between clarity and mystery.

## Short version

Aeloria is the story of a world built from refugee memory, held together by a promise no one can fully remember.
