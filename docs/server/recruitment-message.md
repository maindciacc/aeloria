# Recruitment Copy for Discord

## Short version

Aeloria is a story-led Minecraft worldbuilding and server project built around a forgotten world, a living tree, a promise no one can fully remember, and the peoples who survived the Hunt.

We are building a world with lore, architecture, mystery, and culture. We are looking for builders, writers, lorekeepers, artists, worldbuilders, testers, and people who want to help shape a living place rather than a generic server.

## Welcome message draft

> Welcome to Aeloria.
>
> Before there was The Forgotten Ring, there was Aeloria.
>
> A barren shore. Endless mushrooms. A silent tree. A world that remembers more than it can say.
>
> We are building a world shaped by memory, myth, and survival. The Hunt drove the peoples from their homes. The Isle became a refuge. The Promise was spoken. The roots beneath the world remember more than the people do.
>
> This server is not a random Minecraft hub. It is a world project built around lore, territory, and the slow unveiling of the truth.
>
> We are looking for:
> - builders
> - lore writers and scribes
> - artists and world designers
> - moderators and community helpers
> - testers and world explorers
> - people who want to help shape the story and the place
>
> If you are drawn to strange forests, buried roots, forgotten history, and a world with a heartbeat beneath it, you are in the right place.
>
> Choose your role. Introduce yourself. Explore the lore. Help build the world.

## Recruitment rule

Do not promise a finished server. Promise a world in motion.

That is a more honest and more compelling call.

## Suggested Discord call to action

> Read the lore. Pick a role. Tell us what you want to build. Tell us what you want to uncover.

That is enough to begin.
