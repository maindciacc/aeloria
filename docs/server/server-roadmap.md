# Server Roadmap

## Purpose

Define how the world, the community, and the gameplay layers evolve together without losing the story.

## Phase 1 — Preparation

- Finalize the canon register.
- Confirm the map and territory structure.
- Define who is allowed to write lore, build, and moderate.
- Review Discord onboarding and permission flow.

## Phase 2 — Foundation play

- Prepare the first world milestone.
- Open a limited-membership testing flow.
- Confirm the arrival route, the central tree, and the first mystery discovery path.

## Phase 3 — Build the world

- Complete the main territory builds.
- Add the arrival point, central region, and initial settlements.
- Ensure each people has a meaningful place to live and contribute.

## Phase 4 — Lore reveal

- Begin dropping clues, books, and story fragments.
- Place discoveries in ways that reward curiosity and exploration.
- Let the mysteries deepen before fully resolving them.

## Phase 5 — Launch-ready polish

- Review the world for flow, clarity, and playability.
- Finalize the guild and community structure.
- Test permissions, moderation, role flow, and onboarding.

## Phase 6 — Public opening

- Open the world to a wider player pool.
- Run events and guided structure building.
- Keep the lore active and evolving without sacrificing the canon.
