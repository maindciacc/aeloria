THE ARRIVAL OF THE FIRST FOLK

Dwarven Record. Four words to an entry, when possible. The Elf counted.

— 2 —

Day 1. Landed. Mushroom soil.

Day 2. Nothing grows.

Day 3. Dug.

— 3 —

Day 4. Foreman struck rock. Rock rang.

Day 5. Foreman struck again. Rock answered.

— 4 —

Day 6. Hum. Not the thread. Deeper.

Day 7. Sent for the Elves. Did not tell them why.

— 5 —

Day 8. Elves arrived. Brought lanterns, ink and questions.

Day 9. Told them to stay close. They did not.

— 6 —

Day 12. Satyrs landed. Brought wine. Rock hummed louder.

Day 13. Gnomes. Measured the hum. Numbers disagreed.

— 7 —

Day 20. All seven peoples ashore. Merfolk in the surf.

Day 21. Everyone dreamed it again.

Day 22. Everybody said the word in their sleep.

— 8 —

Day 23. Dug.

Day 24. Dug.

Day 25. Foreman came up out of the hole and said one sentence.

"It knows we're here."
