## 14. THE SILENT CHAMBER

**Game title:** Silent Chamber\
**Place:** Deep roots, Root Chronicles

THE SILENT CHAMBER

Elven account. Dwarven marginalia. Gnomish footnotes, of which the Gnomes are very proud.

— 2 —

It was not quiet. Quiet is the absence of noise.

This was the absence of return.

A dwarf shouted at the entrance and heard his voice leave him and not come back.

— 3 —

The Elves, who value sound above all things, found it unbearable.

One began humming to test it. She stopped when she realised she could no longer hear herself.

— 4 —

&#91;Margin: "Elf has found a room where it cannot talk. Elf is distressed. We are considering moving in."\]

— 5 —

The Gnomes were sent for. They measured the floor. They could not find the ceiling. The distance across and the distance back did not agree.

&#91;Footnote 14 (see 9): The room is larger on the return. Verified three times.\]

— 6 —

At the centre, the Monolith.

The hum is there. You do not hear it with your ears.

You hear it in your teeth.

In the small hard places where a person keeps what they do not say.

— 7 —

Every visitor leaves the same way.

They do not feel they have been listened to.

They feel they have been recognised.

— 8 —

Only one visitor reported nothing at all.

He walked the length of the Chamber, ate half a flower, and asked the Gnomes if they had any cheese.

The hum went quiet as he passed.

It began again behind him. Quieter.
