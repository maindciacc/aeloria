## 13. BENEATH THE ROOTS

**Game title:** Beneath Roots\
**Place:** Mid roots, Root Chronicles. Last book before the shelves thin out.

BENEATH THE ROOTS

The first Elven survey of the root system. Annotated by a Dwarf who was less impressed than the Elf wished.

— 2 —

The Elves went down after the Dwarves, as they did with everything.

Lanterns, ink, and an unreasonable number of questions.

They were told to stay close. They did not.

— 3 —

The first survey took nine weeks. Every Dwarf who saw the map called it wrong.

It showed the roots as a web. A tangle. As any tree grows.

&#91;Margin: "Wrong."\]

— 4 —

The Dwarves took them down again and made them count.

Seven great roots. Not tangled. Not crossing. Each thicker than a watchtower, the colour of old amethyst where the lantern touched.

— 5 —

Seven roots, said the Elves. Seven peoples.

&#91;Archivist: I was very pleased with this. I have since crossed it out.\]

— 6 —

We had forgotten to count the eighth.

Thin. Pale. Nearly invisible.

It did not descend. It ran sideways from the base of the Tree, across the dark, and into the sea.

— 7 —

A merfolk guide, asked about it, did not turn her head.

"That one is ours," she said.

"It does not go down. It goes out."

"It is still listening."

— 8 —

&#91;Margin: "Elf has counted wrong. Elf is upset. We are not telling it about the ninth."\]

&#91;Archivist: What ninth?\]

&#91;Margin: "Count again."\]
