## 12. THE DEEPDELVERS

**Game title:** Deepdelvers\
**Place:** Dwarven Deep Records, upper roots

THE DEEPDELVERS

Dwarven Deep Records. Elven annotations in brackets. Dwarven corrections in the margins, where they have won.

— 2 —

They were not an order at first.

They were eleven stubborn dwarves with nowhere better to be.

Garrun, the eldest, called them the ones who go down.

Others shortened it. Deepdelvers.

— 3 —

By the end of the first year there were four hundred.

Above the tunnel mouth, in chalk, was a rule.

WRITE LESS.

— 4 —

&#91;Archivist: I assumed this was a joke. It is the first law of the Deepdelvers.\]

&#91;Margin: "Elf thinks we are lazy. We are careful."\]

— 5 —

The reason is recorded once, in the journal of Brunn, who wrote it and never wrote again.

"Things written down here get read."

— 6 —

So they wrote "Dug." instead of "We dug the north wall and found copper."

Every sentence not written was a sentence nobody could read.

— 7 —

Then a young dwarf wrote a full page, every detail of what he had seen below.

It was found next morning on the tunnel floor.

Neatly folded.

Corrected.

— 8 —

The Elf asked Garrun what the forges and the armory were for.

"Prepare," he said.

"For what?"

He pointed down.

Then, more slowly, he pointed up.
