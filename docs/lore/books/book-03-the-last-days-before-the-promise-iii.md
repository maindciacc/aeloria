## 3. THE LAST DAYS BEFORE THE PROMISE III

**Game title:** Last Days III\
**Place:** HIDDEN. A drowned ship in a deep Overworld ocean, in a barrel. (Ties to the hunted fleet.)

THE LAST DAYS BEFORE THE PROMISE III

From the weather diary of a Sylph who flew too high.

— 2 —

Day 1. Clear. Wind from the east.

Day 2. Clear. A fleet below. Seven hulls. Refugees.

Day 3. Clear. The Hunt is following them. It does not use ships.

— 3 —

Day 4. The sea ahead of the fleet is calm. The sea behind it has gone still.

Not calm. Still.

I do not have a word for the difference.

— 4 —

Day 6. A sailor looked up and saw me. She did not wave.

She mouthed three words. I have flown low enough to read them.

"Do not follow."

— 5 —

Day 7. I followed. Of course I followed. I am a Sylph.

The wind began to push me off course. Gently. Steadily. Away from the fleet.

As if it too had decided.

— 6 —

Day 8. Fog. The fleet was in it. So was something else.

It was warm. It smelled of cut stone.

Day 9. No fleet.

— 7 —

Day 10. Wind from the west, for the first time in my life.

It is carrying me toward land that is on no map.

It is carrying me home.

I have never been there.
