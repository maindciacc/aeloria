## 10. FIRST SIGHT OF YDRASIL

**Game title:** First Sight\
**Place:** Canopy, Root Chronicles, near the entrance (a good first hook)

FIRST SIGHT OF YDRASIL

Written by the first Elf ashore. She was not the first to arrive. She was the first to look up.

— 2 —

We came ashore at dawn, and the first thing we saw was nothing.

A purple plain. Red mushrooms scattered like coins.

— 3 —

Then I looked up.

A Tree. Black bark, amethyst in its veins. Taller than a watchtower.

Not one leaf on it. In a land that grew only mushrooms.

— 4 —

I assumed it was dead.

The Dwarves, already at its base with their ears to the bark, said nothing.

I have since learned that Dwarves lie by omission.

— 5 —

Beneath it sat a villager in a green robe, eating a flower.

He had been there, he told me later, for some time.

He did not say how long.

— 6 —

&#91;Archivist: This is the only account in which the Tree is bare. Every later account describes it in bloom. I believe the author. I do not believe the later accounts. I cannot say why.\]

— 7 —

I asked the villager what the Tree was called.

He looked at me for a very long time.

"Hrmm."

And the Tree, very softly, hummed back.
