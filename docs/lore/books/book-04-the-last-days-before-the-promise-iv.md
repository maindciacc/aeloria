THE LAST DAYS BEFORE THE PROMISE IV

From the Dwarven Record. Copied by an Elf who has stopped adding notes. \[The Elf has been asked to.\]

— 2 —

Day 1. Stonereach. Gates shut.

Day 2. Gates held.

Day 3. Gates held.

— 3 —

Day 9. Food low. Hunt at the walls. Does not attack. Does not leave.

Day 10. Foreman asked what the Hunt wants. Nobody knew. Dug a well anyway.

— 4 —

Day 14. Well found a thread of gold. Thin as hair. Running down.

Day 15. Foreman said follow it.

Nobody asked why.

— 5 —

Day 20. Whole Sanctuary followed the thread. Four hundred dwarves. Left the gates open.

Hunt did not follow into the dark.

Day 21. Dug.

— 6 —

Day 31. Sea above us. No water through the stone.

Day 32. Dug.

Day 40. The rock started humming.

Day 41. Nobody will admit they can hear it.

Day 42. Everybody is humming.

— 7 —

Day 43. The thread has stopped going down.

It is going up.
