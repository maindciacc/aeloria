## 7. DREAMS ACROSS THE WORLD

**Game title:** Dreams Across\
**Place:** Fairy Collection, upper roots (a song, so it reads as a lullaby)

DREAMS ACROSS THE WORLD

A Fairy song, set down by an Elf who cannot sing and has apologised to the song.

— 2 —

Seven nights I dreamed a shape, A shape that had no name. Seven nights a word went by And left me not the same.

— 3 —

Where the roots go deep and wide, Past amethyst and bone, There's a door that is not a door And a stone that is not stone.

— 4 —

Every child had sung it. Every land. No one taught them. No one wrote it. It was simply in the air. Like a name you have not spoken.

— 5 —

&#91;Archivist: The Fairies sing this to children as a lullaby. I laid the verses over a map. They do not describe a route. They describe the Isle, and where the roots go, and in what order. This song was sung before the Isle existed.\]

— 6 —

Count the roots and count them twice, And one of them will call.

— 7 —

The Fairies say there is a fourth verse.

Asked to sing it, they stop, and look at the floor.

The Elf asked three times.

The third time a Fairy answered.

"It isn't finished."
