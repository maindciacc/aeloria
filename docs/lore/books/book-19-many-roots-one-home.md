## 19. MANY ROOTS, ONE HOME

**Game title:** Many Roots\
**Place:** Satyr Tales shelf

MANY ROOTS, ONE HOME

The Satyr account of the First Feast. The Gnomish account differs by forty guests. The Archivist chose not to resolve this.

— 2 —

The first winter was the hardest.

Seven peoples, the merfolk in the water, one king, on an Isle that had only just decided to be an Isle.

The Elves had the library. The Dwarves had the deep. Neither would say what they wanted of the other.

— 3 —

&#91;Margin: "Elf wanted us to sign something. We signed it. It said nothing. Elf was thrilled."\]

The treaty that held was not a treaty.

It was a meal.

— 4 —

Every people brought what they had.

Mushroom stew from the Elves. Stone-baked bread from the Dwarves. Honey cakes from the Fairies, who dropped half from the air. Smoked meat from the Gnomes, labelled by weight.

— 5 —

Fish and cheap wine from the Satyrs, who brought the most and complained the least. Wind-dried fruit from the Sylphs, who ate theirs first.

Salt, and one black bowl of something nobody asked about, from the Darklings.

— 6 —

They ate in the open grass between territories, with no walls and no guards.

Nothing was decided.

Everything was settled.

— 7 —

Near the end, the king, whichever one, tried the black bowl.

The Darklings went very still.

"Hrmm," said Hughbert.

And he finished it. All of it.

— 8 —

One Darkling stayed and cleaned the bowl. It is the first time the Records show a Darkling cleaning something that was not a secret.

Nobody has learned what was in it.

Hughbert has never been sick.
