## 9. THE JOURNEY BEYOND MAPS

**Game title:** Beyond Maps\
**Place:** HIDDEN. Overworld, in a cartography-table chest on a lone ocean island.

THE JOURNEY BEYOND MAPS

Sylph Sky Archives. Written by three navigators who disagree on the heading throughout.

— 2 —

Navigator A: The chart is blank.

Navigator B: The chart is not blank. The chart has been erased.

Navigator C: These are the same thing.

— 3 —

A: Heading north.

B: Heading north.

C: We are not heading north. We are being headed.

— 4 —

Day 3. The stars changed. Not moved. Changed.

We named the new shapes anyway. It is the only honest thing a navigator can do.

— 5 —

Day 5. The sea went flat as glass. Our wakes did not spread.

A: They are not wakes.

B: They are scars.

C: We are being written on.

— 6 —

Day 8. Fog. In the fog, a low shore. Pale purple. Red caps along the sand.

B: There is no harbour.

A: Then why does the wind keep pushing us in?

— 7 —

The wind pushed the whole fleet onto the beach. Behind us the sea shut like a door.

Navigator C wrote one word under the coastline.

"Home."

She had never seen it.

None of us disagreed.
