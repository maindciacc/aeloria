# Root Chronicles Index

## Purpose

The Root Chronicles are the memory-archive of Aeloria. They are written in many voices. They disagree. That disagreement is not a flaw. It is part of the world.

## Major themes

- Many roots, one home
- Survival and refuge
- Memory and forgetting
- The cost of the Promise
- The difference between what is remembered and what is true

## Key locations

- **Ydrasil** — the great tree at the heart of the Isle
- **Arcane Grove** — Elven settlement and old refuge
- **Deeproot** — beneath the world, discovered by the Dwarves
- **Silent Chamber** — site of the Monolith and the Promise
- **Human Harbour** — present in the map and historical references, but not fully resolved in canon
- **Ring of Monuments** — merfolk sea-bound remains and ancient watchpoints

## Narrative voices

- Elven archivists and scholars
- Dwarven record and margin notes
- Fairy songs and memory-lore
- Satyr stories and tavern truth
- Sylph navigators and sky journals
- Merfolk records of currents and cold thresholds
- Gnomish measurement, observation, and footnotes
- Darkling accounts of silence and hidden knowledge

## Provenance note

The Chronicles are fragments. Some are recovered. Some are hidden. Some are deliberately incomplete. The world benefits from uncertainty. The project should keep that uncertainty alive whenever possible.
