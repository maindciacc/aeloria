# World Build Roadmap

## Goal

Turn the world into a playable, explorable, and discoverable island where lore and environment support one another.

## Phase 1 — Foundation and arrival

- Build the island edge and world border feel.
- Establish the arrival point and the first impression of the Isle.
- Define the initial route from shore to the core.
- Frame the first mystery: the tree, mushrooms, silence, and missing memory.

## Phase 2 — Peoples and territories

- Assign each territory a visual identity.
- Add pathing, landmarks, and cultural objects.
- Establish homes or structures for each people in their region.
- Add functionally important locations: farms, workshops, gathering places, and roads.

## Phase 3 — The root network

- Build out the central root structure and visible crossings.
- Add routes to and from the underworld entrances.
- Use root imagery as both practical travel and thematic structure.

## Phase 4 — Hidden archaeology and lore

- Place the Silent Chamber route.
- Build the Monolith space and surrounding ritual geography.
- Add hidden or partially sealed spaces beneath the island.
- Place clues, books, and environmental hints for discoveries.

## Phase 5 — The Promise and the deeper mysteries

- Build the reveal structure for the Promise and its aftermath.
- Introduce the Empty Pedestal and its unanswered role.
- Establish points of contradiction where players can sense a truth beyond the records.

## Phase 6 — Playtest and expansion

- Test flow, pacing, visibility, and usability.
- Confirm the world can be explored without losing narrative weight.
- Expand inward toward the hidden roots, deeper chambers, and the final historical reveal.
