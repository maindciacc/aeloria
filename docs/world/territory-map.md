# Aeloria World Map Overview

## Project status

The current territory map is a working world plan, not yet final canon. It defines the structure of the Isle and shows how each people may occupy their own domain.

## Core world structure

The Isle is divided into territorial zones, each associated with a people and a role:

- Elves — Arcane Grove, library and root-knowledge
- Fairies — floating homes, bridges, and botanical growth
- Gnomes — workshops, tunnels, tools, and crafting
- Darklings — shadow enclosures, silence, and sealed knowledge
- Satyrs — coast, fishing, trade, and open settlements
- Sylphs — sky platforms, routefinding, and observation
- Dwarves — deep roots, forges, unbroken stone, and the underworld
- Human Harbour — possible arrival point, needs lore resolution

## Notes on the map

- The island is large, with defined borders and internal terrain transitions.
- The central tree region is grounded and mythic.
- The roots are not merely decorative. They are the world’s memory-structure.
- The map strongly suggests each people has a specific relationship to the land itself.
- The deeper regions below the island are likely central to discovery or to the true heart of the Promise.

## Build directions

- Keep the central tree and root system visible and symbolic.
- The harbour should feel like a threshold rather than a normal village.
- Each people should have a recognizable visual and cultural identity.
- The world should reward exploration, not only travel by straight line.

## Unresolved map questions

- Does the Human Harbour belong to canon or to the player-facing arrival narrative?
- Are the territory borders final, or are they intentionally porous?
- Which routes lead to Deeproot, the Silent Chamber, and the deeper mysteries?
- Is the map intended to be a player-facing guide or the hidden map of a historical world?
