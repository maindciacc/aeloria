# Canon Register

> What is approved. What is draft. What remains unresolved.

## Source status legend

- **Approved**: canon-level material that is currently treated as the project’s working truth.
- **Draft**: material with strong worldbuilding value, but still under revision.
- **Reference**: useful supporting material; not necessarily canon.
- **Open question**: unresolved contradiction or missing detail.

## Core approved foundations

### World and setting

- The world is Aeloria.
- The Isle is the refuge place after the Hunt.
- The Hunt drove the peoples from their homes.
- The Promise was spoken before the Monolith in the Silent Chamber.
- The Isle was shaped after the Promise.
- Ydrasil is the central tree.
- Deeproot lies beneath the Isle.
- The Ring of Monuments exists beneath the sea.

### Peoples

- Fairies
- Elves
- Dwarves
- Satyrs
- Gnomes
- Darklings
- Sylphs
- Merfolk

### Major mysteries

- The Hunt
- The Child with the Lantern
- The Fourth Verse
- The Ninth Root
- The Empty Pedestal
- The One Who Remembers
- Hughbert and the Crown

## Draft material in active use

- Root Chronicles books 1-20, as written in the draft file
- The territory map and labels from the provided world map references
- The Discord server structure and welcome onboarding content from the screenshots

## Open questions

1. Is the Human Harbour canon, or is it a player-facing or transitional concept?
2. How exactly do the territory boundaries align with the root system, the sea, and the cryptic lore?
3. What is the deeper origin of the peoples, and how does Aeloria fit into the wider world beyond the Isle?
4. What exactly is the nature of the Promise and why do every people remember it differently?
5. What is the Ninth Root, and why is it hidden or omitted from the records?
6. What exactly was spoken before the Monolith? No surviving record remains.
7. Who or what is the One Who Remembers, and why does the Empty Pedestal wait for them?
